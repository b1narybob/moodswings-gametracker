How to Play

The Basics

The goal is to win three rounds. A round is won by having the highest total "score" on the table at the end of the round. The deck has 45 cards, each representing an emotion or mental state with a specific value (the die symbol).

  • White Dice: The card's value is fixed and will not change.
  • Black Dice: The card's value can change based on the state of the game, so check its rules text.
  • Secondary Value: Some cards have a second value in the lower-left corner; if a card changes to this value, rotate it 180 degrees.

Setting Up

  • Shuffle and Deal: Shuffle the shared 45-card deck. Deal exactly five cards to each player.
  • Determine First Player: Reveal the bottom card of the deck. The player who most recently felt that specific emotion goes first.

Taking a Turn

During a round, players take turns in clockwise order. On your turn, you must play exactly one card from your hand face-up onto the table, or pass.

Cards in play are called moods. Resolve a played card in this exact order:

  • To play this card: A cost you must pay before placing the card down.
  • While in play: A continuous effect applying as long as it is on the table.
  • After playing this mood: An action taken immediately after playing the card.

Scoring the Round

After every player has taken one turn, the round ends:

  • Calculate Scores: Each player adds up the current values of all their moods. Highest score wins. Tiebreaker: The tied player who took their turn earliest in the round wins.
  • After-Scoring Effects: Resolve any cards that trigger specifically after scoring.
  • Draw Cards: The winner does NOT draw. Every player who lost the round draws one card.
  • Hurt Feelings: In a 3-4 player game, the player(s) with the absolute lowest score may play an extra card on their turn next round.

The winner of the round takes the first turn in the next round. First to win 3 rounds wins the game.
